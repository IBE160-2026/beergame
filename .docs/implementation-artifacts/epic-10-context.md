# Epic 10 Context: Replay

<!-- Generated from planning artifacts. Regenerate with compile-epic-context if planning docs change. -->

## Goal

After passing the Debrief quiz, a Student plays one Replay alone, in the same Role, against three AI players, under a Replay scenario that differs per Student (harder than the Class game, not playable from memory). It runs on the existing play screen with an added projection line, ends with Total cost, and is followed by a short walkthrough comparing it with the Student's own game. A Lecturer can keep the default Replay scenario or set every Game setting. The epic owns `backend/app/modules/replay/` and `frontend/src/modules/replay/`, creates `purpose=replay` Games through `games.commands`, and reuses Epic 6's play screen and Epic 9's facts, renderer and validator.

## Stories

- Story 10.1: Get a default Replay scenario that differs per Student
- Story 10.2: Set the Replay scenario freely
- Story 10.3: Start the Replay after passing the Debrief quiz
- Story 10.4: Play the Replay with the projection line
- Story 10.5: Finish the Replay and unlock the next step
- Story 10.6: Close unfinished Replays at the Test deadline
- Story 10.7: Read the Replay walkthrough
- Story 10.8: Rate how believable the AI players were
- Story 10.9: Let Information visibility differ in the Replay (cut list, behind `FEATURE_INFO_VISIBILITY`)

## Requirements & Constraints

- A Student who has passed the Debrief quiz can start the Replay any time before their Test deadline, in the same Role as in their own game, with three AI players in the Class session's AI mode. They can leave and resume on any device and wait only for AI answers. A Student with no Seat in the Class session (no game of their own) gets no Replay. A finished make-up game counts as the Student's own game; a make-up closed at the Test deadline never does.
- Default scenario (Classic step): Class demand levels (4, then 8) with per-Week discrete uniform variation, spread 2; step Week ranged 5-12; Weeks ranged 30-40; no Guided Weeks; everything else as the Class session. Other demand kinds: step keeps its levels with spread 2 or the Class spread if larger; random keeps the mean, spread at least 2, no step Week; a fixed series becomes per-Week levels with spread 2, last value held. Demand never below 0. Step Week, Week count and future demand stay hidden during play (axis stops at nå + 4). Every Replay draws a fresh demand seed per Student, stored with the Game.
- A Lecturer can accept the default or set every Game setting (prefilled from the Class session's), including the Class game's exact settings. In the form only Weeks and the change Week take ranges; chain, capacity and Information visibility follow the Game settings. Non-blocking warnings when a change likely makes the Replay easier than the Class game: shorter delay, lower peak demand, smaller spread, change of level removed, any Guided Weeks, higher starting inventory (a range counts by its easier end); the list is one tested function in `modules/sessions`. Settings lock at the Start date (`409 session_locked`); Assistant lecturers read only.
- Replay end: the last Week runs in full with no earlier hint, then «Replay er ferdig», Totalkostnad (own only), «Gå til gjennomgangen». Finishing records the `replay` step once, idempotently, which unlocks the Individual test. The finished-game check (engine replay of stored Orders must reproduce stored Week states) runs for Replays too, from the sweeper, so up to a minute after the Replay ends.
- Walkthrough: Total cost, a run chart, a comparison with the Student's own game on one axis, one or two specific moments from the Student's own Orders; every number matches the stored Week rows. It must say «Ikke like for like.» (AI neighbours, different demand, possibly shorter run) and never rank, praise or declare a better run. AI text is prefixed «KI-generert, kan ta feil:». With fewer than 10 own Orders in their own game, show at least three Replay moments and omit the Order comparison and Class game total, stating the Class game was mostly AI-played. Code-rendered fallback when Debrief AI text is off, rejected or slow.
- Two optional, never blocking questions after the walkthrough: believability (five-point) and wanting another round (yes/no). The believability question is also offered once on «Spillet er ferdig», only if an AI player held some Class game Seat in some Week; a Lecturer's test Game never asks. Each is asked until answered, then one thank-you line replaces it. Ratings are never shown to staff beside a Student name and are returned only to the Student who gave them; aggregation belongs to Epic 14.
- Unfinished Replays are closed (not played out by AI) at the Student's Test deadline, judged on the app's clock; no `replay` step is recorded and the Decision log stays intact. A closed Replay has no walkthrough, no result, no facts set, no finished-game check and no rating prompt; from the deadline on, the Student's Order answers `409 test_deadline_passed` even before the sweep closes it (an AI player's Order may still complete a Week the Student ordered in time, until the close). A Replay stored but never started is closed too.
- All screens pass axe at 320/360/1440 px (200 % text); all copy from `frontend/src/copy/nb.ts` (draft copy is built as written; the founder corrects it after seeing the site); charts have «Vis som tabell».

## Technical Decisions

- Template model (closes the spine's deferred entry): `engine.ReplayTemplate` (Pydantic, `schema_version` 1) holds every `GameSettings` field as a whole-number fixed value or `IntRange`, plus demand as a level pattern (`step` with ranged change Week, `random`, or `fixed`) with a spread. `engine.replay_settings(template, seed) -> GameSettings` is the one pure resolver (no I/O, no clock): each ranged value is drawn from a SHA-256 of the seed and field name, and the seed becomes the settings' `demand_seed` so the series comes from `demand.series`. `engine.default_replay_template(settings)` derives the default, one rule per demand kind; `engine.validate_template` runs `engine.validate` on corner draws; `engine.replay_template_admits` tells the Play guard whether settings are a draw of a calibrated template.
- The Class session stores only the choice (`class_sessions.replay_scenario`: default or own) plus a JSONB own template; the default is never stored as a copy. A pin provider at `sessions.lock` freezes the template into `SessionRuntime.pins["replay_template"]`; Replays read only that. Copy-from (Story 5.18) also copies the scenario when `FEATURE_RERUN` is on.
- Replay Games snapshot at creation: `GameSettings`, demand series, seed, `rules_version` and the Class session's `SessionRuntime` (via `sessions.service.build_snapshot`); only settings, series and seed are the Replay's own. `games.commands.create` may need extending to take a Class session id and a ready snapshot (do not rely on cut-list Story 6.20). Replays in a Control test Class session use the ordinary `replay` purpose. While the cost-limit state is set, new AI Seats are `rule_based` with reason `cost_limit`. Replay at an older `rules_version` is an open spine item (decide before the first engine change after a real Game is stored); never alter engine output for stored Games.
- `games.policy` gets a `replay` row: no inactivity Takeover, no Pause, no Play deadline playout, Guided Weeks only if the scenario sets them, not on the Lecturer overview, snapshot at creation, does not record the Class game step, pre-play gate off, a new `projection` column (on only for replay) and the `closes_at_test_deadline` behaviour. `ops.sweep` skips replay Games for Takeover and playout. Commands, sweeps and views ask policy, never branch on purpose.
- `modules/replay` writes no game table and imports other slices only via their `service.py`. Role and own game come from `games.service.student_game(user, class_session)`; seats via `games.commands.assign_seat`, then `start`; Orders only via `place_order`. A unique key on Student and Class session makes start idempotent.
- Gates and steps via `progress.has_step` / `progress.record_step` (`quiz_passed` gate, `replay` step; `403 quiz_required` without the quiz); Test deadline only via `sessions.service.effective_test_deadline` (start refused `409 test_deadline_passed`).
- `games.commands.close(game, reason)` (added to the AD-2 command list) ends an unfinished Game as `finished` with `end_reason = test_deadline` (new column on `games`, empty for `finish`), no last Week, no finish hook; locks the Game row, bumps `version`, emits `game:<id>` and `user:<id>`; idempotent, ids only, registry-tested; only purposes whose policy row allows it (the Replay). Driven by a new AD-9 sweep due action (`games.close_at_test_deadline`). `place_order` and running `ai-orders` jobs get `409 test_deadline_passed` and end without an Order or retry. `end_reason` is returned through `games.service` so reports never treat a closed Replay as finished. The Game data export includes Replays with a purpose column.
- Projection value is display arithmetic in `frontend/src/lib/preview.ts` from `SeatState` fields plus the typed Order; missing fields are added to `games.schemas.SeatState`; Vitest checks against engine golden values. The delay horizon is the Order's lead time (order delay plus shipping or production delay), since the Lecturer can change delays; with nothing typed the Order counts as 0 and the sentence says so; a horizon past nå + 4 keeps the axis at nå + 4 (line to last column, no dot, sentence still gives the value at nå + horizon).
- Walkthrough: facts from `games.facts.get_or_create` (unchanged `analysis.facts`), moments rendered by `analysis.render`; the walkthrough record stores both `fact_set_id`s. Narration is one idempotent `llm-batch` job keyed by the Replay's `fact_set_id` via the shared `app/ai` helper (Story 9.14, imports only `platform`) with purpose `replay_walkthrough`, taking `analysis.validate_ai_text` and a code renderer as arguments. Versioned prompt under `backend/app/ai/prompts/replay_walkthrough/`; a new immutable config row (Story 2.2 command) adds the purpose. Pending state refetches on `user:<id>`, then falls back to code after a configured wait (placeholder 45 seconds after the finish, `REPLAY_WALKTHROUGH_WAIT_SECONDS`); the line a Student first sees stays. The line tying moments together cites only the two moments ranked first (Detected pattern first, then higher later cost).
- Simulation: `python -m app.sim` runs on resolved default Replay settings; a `replay_default` profile in a new Play guard parameter set version makes `analysis.play_guard.is_calibrated` count the default Replay as calibrated and own scenarios as not.
- Ratings: believability stored once per Student and Game through `games.service` (with purpose and AI mode; a second answer is `409`); another-round answer once per Student and Class session by `modules/replay`; tables created by this epic.
- Staff reach a Replay's Spillinnsikt (titled «Replay») from the Student's Seat on the Class game's Spillinnsikt; it has no Messages, Explanation or Debrief links.
- Information visibility (Story 10.9) depends on `FEATURE_INFO_VISIBILITY` and Story 5.16; it only extends the template, form, warning list and snapshot; no earlier story depends on it.

## UX & Interaction Patterns

- Klasseøkt Replay block: Valgknapper switch default and own scenario; the default is described in words (hidden values stay hidden); the form reuses Epic 5's Game settings fields and inline range validation; one column at narrow widths; read-only after the Start date, no edit controls for assistants.
- Stegene wording: locked «Replay, åpnes når du har bestått Debrief-quizen.»; in progress «Fortsett Replay, Uke 9.»; AI-held Seats named «KI-spiller» from first view; finished shows done plus next step available. A closed Replay reads «stengt ved Testfristen, ikke fullført» (Stegene) and is never shown as finished; a never-started Replay reads «ikke gjort, Testfristen er ute».
- Hjem: «Start Replay» sits under the Class game once the quiz is passed, with a consequence line above, and is the primary button when no game action is; it opens the Replay's play screen at once. While running, Hjem's Replay block has «Fortsett Replay, Uke 9» (the way there; the Stegene step is no second link). A finished Replay is reopened via «Se Replay». Refusal lines for no quiz and past Testfrist.
- The Replay uses the Epic 6 play screen unchanged (Kjedebånd, Graf over tabell, Ordrefelt, Setestatus), with no Pausevalg, «Sett inn KI-spiller», time proposal, Starting task or Debrief link; the briefing and screen state the Replay's own delays and rates; «Grossist, Replay» under the Uke; AI rows show «venter». Only the Replay adds the dashed `projection` line ending in a hollow dot at nå + 4, with the sentence «Den stiplede linja viser netto lager om fire uker: 3 + 24 − 4 × 8 = −5 hvis ordre inn holder seg. Ikke garantert.» (with nothing typed: «… 3 − 4 × 8 = −29 hvis du ikke bestiller og ordre inn holder seg. Ikke garantert.»). The line is `aria-hidden`, distinguished by dash and marker, not colour alone; the sentence is the text alternative, keeps its sum on one line (non-breaking spaces, breaking only where a line is narrower than the sum) and wraps without overlap at 320 px. Net graph direct labels move to the nearest height clear of the projection line and dot, the Netto lager label first; where one line leaves room for only one label, the other keeps its height with a halo.
- Finish screen: Totalkostnad only (no Kjedens regning, no chart), one primary «Gå til gjennomgangen» leading to `/spill/<id>/gjennomgang`, shared number formatter. A closed Replay's play screen reads «Replay stengt» with two lines and «Til Hjem», no button forward; Spillinnsikt shows «Stengt ved Testfristen, ikke fullført» in place of «Ferdig».
- Walkthrough («Replay-gjennomgang», over «Grossist, Replay»): «Totalkostnad, Replay» beside «Totalkostnad, Klassespillet (Spill N)», one chart of Netto lager for both runs on the same Uke axis (coloured Replay, grey Class game, with «Vis som tabell»), «Ikke like for like.» with its reasons, then «Øyeblikk fra din Replay» with the tying line and the two top moments. States: pending «Gjennomgangen lages.» in a Skjelett of the chart; ready; code fallback with the same chart and numbers; before the Replay ends «Gjennomgangen kommer når Replay er ferdig.» with «Til Replay».
- Questions: believability «Hvor troverdige var KI-spillernes ordrer?» (1 to 5, end points labelled in words, «Send svar» secondary) and «Ville du spilt en runde til?» (Ja, Nei), under «Frivillige spørsmål» after the walkthrough, or «Et frivillig spørsmål» under «Gå til Debrief» on «Spillet er ferdig». The scale is a labelled radio group, keyboard operable, options at least 44 px. A closed Replay asks nothing.
