# Epic 10 Context: Replay

<!-- Generated from planning artifacts. Regenerate with compile-epic-context if planning docs change. -->

## Goal

After passing the Debrief quiz, a Student plays one Replay alone, in the same Role, against three AI players, under a Replay scenario that differs per Student (harder than the Class game, not playable from memory). It runs on the existing play screen with an added projection line, ends with Total cost, and is followed by a short walkthrough comparing it with the Student's own game. A Lecturer can keep the default Replay scenario or set every Game setting. The epic owns `backend/app/modules/replay/` and `frontend/src/modules/replay/`, creates `purpose=replay` Games through `games.commands`, and reuses Epic 6's play screen and Epic 9's facts, renderer and validator.

## Stories

- Story 10.1: Get a default Replay scenario that differs per Student
- Story 10.2: Set the Replay scenario freely
- Story 10.3: Start the Replay after passing the Debrief quiz
- Story 10.4: Play the Replay with the projection line
- Story 10.5: Finish the Replay and unlock the next step
- Story 10.6: Close unfinished Replays at the Test deadline
- Story 10.7: Read the Replay walkthrough
- Story 10.8: Rate how believable the AI players were
- Story 10.9: Let Information visibility differ in the Replay (cut list, behind `FEATURE_INFO_VISIBILITY`)

## Requirements & Constraints

- A Student who has passed the Debrief quiz can start the Replay any time before their Test deadline, in the same Role as in their own game, with three AI players in the Class session's AI mode. They can leave and resume on any device; nobody else holds them up, they wait only for AI answers.
- Default scenario (Classic step): Class demand levels (4, then 8) with per-Week discrete uniform variation, spread 2; step Week ranged 5-12; Weeks ranged 30-40; no Guided Weeks; everything else as the Class session. Other demand kinds: step keeps its levels with spread 2 or the Class spread if larger; random keeps the mean, spread at least 2, no step Week; a fixed series becomes per-Week levels with spread 2, last value held. Demand never below 0. Step Week, Week count and future demand stay hidden during play (axis stops at nå + 4). Every Replay draws a fresh demand seed per Student, stored with the Game.
- A Lecturer can accept the default or set every Game setting (prefilled from the Class session's), including the Class game's exact settings. In the form only Weeks and the change Week take ranges; the chain, capacity and Information visibility follow the Game settings. Non-blocking warnings for changes likely to make the Replay easier versus the Class game: shorter delay, lower peak demand, smaller spread, change of level removed, any Guided Weeks, higher starting inventory (a range counts by its easier end). Settings lock at the Start date (`409 session_locked`); assistant lecturers read only.
- Replay end: last Week runs in full with no earlier hint, then «Replay er ferdig», Totalkostnad, «Gå til gjennomgangen». Finishing records the `replay` step once, idempotently, which unlocks the Individual test. The finished-game check (engine replay of stored Orders must reproduce stored Week states) runs for Replays too.
- Walkthrough: Total cost, a run chart, a comparison with the Student's own game on one axis, one or two specific moments from the Student's own Orders; every number matches the stored Week rows. It must say «Ikke like for like.» (AI neighbours, different demand, possibly shorter run) and never rank, praise or declare a better run. AI text is prefixed «KI-generert, kan ta feil:». With fewer than 10 own Orders in their own game, show at least three Replay moments and omit the Order comparison, stating the Class game was mostly AI-played. Code-rendered fallback when Debrief AI text is off, rejected or slow.
- Two optional questions after the walkthrough: believability (five-point) and wanting another round (yes/no); the believability question is also offered once on «Spillet er ferdig» if a Class game Seat was AI-held. Never blocking; ratings never shown to staff beside a Student name, and returned only to the Student who gave them; aggregation is Epic 14.
- Unfinished Replays are closed (not played out by AI) at the Student's Test deadline; no `replay` step is recorded and the Decision log stays intact. A closed Replay has no walkthrough and no rating prompt.
- Accessibility and layout: pass axe at 320/360/1440 px (200 % text), all copy from `frontend/src/copy/nb.ts`, charts have «Vis som tabell».

## Technical Decisions

- Template model (spine's deferred entry closed by Story 10.1): `engine.ReplayTemplate` (Pydantic, `schema_version` 1) holds every `GameSettings` field as a whole number fixed or an `IntRange`, plus demand as a level pattern (`step` with ranged change Week, `random`, or `fixed`) with a spread. `engine.replay_settings(template, seed) -> GameSettings` is the one pure resolver (no I/O, no clock; each ranged value drawn from a hash of the seed and field name; the seed becomes the settings' `demand_seed`, so the series comes from `demand.series`). `engine.default_replay_template(settings)` derives the default, one rule per demand kind; `engine.validate_template` checks corner draws with the same validation as Game settings; `engine.replay_template_admits` tells the Play guard whether settings are a draw of a calibrated template.
- The Class session stores only the choice (`class_sessions.replay_scenario`: `default` or own) plus a JSONB own template; the default is never stored as a copy. A pin provider at `sessions.lock` freezes the template into `SessionRuntime.pins["replay_template"]`; Replays read only that. Copy-from (Story 5.18) also copies the scenario when `FEATURE_RERUN` is on.
- Replay Games snapshot at creation (AD-4): `GameSettings`, demand series, seed, `rules_version`, and the Class session's `SessionRuntime` (via `sessions.service.build_snapshot`); only settings, series and seed are the Replay's own. `games.commands.create` may need extending to take a Class session id and a ready snapshot (not relying on cut-list Story 6.20).
- `games.policy` gets a `replay` row: no inactivity Takeover, no Pause, no Play deadline playout, Guided Weeks only if the scenario sets them, not on the Lecturer overview, snapshot at creation, does not record the Class game step, pre-play gate off, and a new `projection` column (on only for replay). `ops.sweep` skips replay Games for Takeover and playout. Commands, sweeps and views ask policy, never branch on purpose.
- `modules/replay` writes no game table and imports other slices only via their `service.py`. Role and own game come from `games.service.student_game(user, class_session)` (a finished make-up counts as the Student's game); seats via `games.commands.assign_seat`, then `games.commands.start`; orders only via `place_order`. While cost-limit state is set, AI Seats are `rule_based` with reason `cost_limit`. A unique key on Student and Class session makes start idempotent.
- Gates and steps through `progress.has_step` / `progress.record_step(...)` (`quiz_passed` gate, `replay` step); Test deadline only via `sessions.service.effective_test_deadline(user, class_session)` (start refused `409 test_deadline_passed`; `403 quiz_required` without the quiz).
- New command `games.commands.close(game, reason)` plus a new AD-9 sweep due action (spine's deferred entry closed by Story 10.6): locks the Game row, sets `lifecycle = finished` with `end_reason = test_deadline` (new column on `games`, empty for `finish`), opens no Week, bumps `version`, emits `game:<id>` and `user:<id>`; idempotent, ids only, registry-tested. `place_order` and running `ai-orders` jobs get `409 test_deadline_passed` and end without an Order. `end_reason` is returned through `games.service` so reports never treat a closed Replay as finished. The Game data export includes Replays with a purpose column.
- Projection value is display arithmetic in `frontend/src/lib/preview.ts` from `SeatState` fields (Posisjon, incoming order, delay horizon) plus the typed Order; missing fields are added to `games.schemas.SeatState`; Vitest checks against engine-produced golden values. The delay horizon (4 with Classic delays) drives the sentence and the dot column since the Lecturer can change delays.
- Walkthrough: facts from `games.facts.get_or_create` (unchanged `analysis.facts`; payload holds numbers and Roles only), moments rendered by `analysis.render` with pinned templates; the walkthrough record stores both `fact_set_id`s. Narration is one idempotent `llm-batch` job keyed by the Replay's `fact_set_id`, via the shared `app/ai` helper (Story 9.14, imports only `platform`) with purpose `replay_walkthrough`, taking `analysis.validate_ai_text` and a code renderer as arguments. Versioned prompt under `backend/app/ai/prompts/replay_walkthrough/`; a new immutable config row (Story 2.2 command) adds the purpose to the `prompts` map. Page shows a pending state, refetches on `user:<id>`, then falls back to code after a configured wait (placeholder under one minute).
- Simulation: `python -m app.sim` runs on resolved default Replay settings; a `replay_default` profile is added in a new Play guard parameter set version so `analysis.play_guard.is_calibrated` counts the default Replay as calibrated and own scenarios as not.
- Ratings: believability stored once per Student and Game through `games.service` (with purpose and AI mode; second answer `409`); another-round answer stored once per Student and Class session by `modules/replay`; in tables this epic creates.
- Information visibility (Story 10.9) is behind `FEATURE_INFO_VISIBILITY` and Story 5.16; it only extends the template, the form, the warning list and the snapshot; no earlier story depends on it.

## UX & Interaction Patterns

- Klasseøkt Replay block: Valgknapper switch between default and own scenario; the default is described in words (hidden values stay hidden); form reuses the Epic 5 Game settings fields and inline range validation; one column at narrow widths; read-only after Start date, edit controls absent for assistants. Draft copy («Standard», «Eget scenario», range labels, warning lines) is added to `nb.ts` for founder review.
- Stegene wording: locked «Replay, åpnes når du har bestått Debrief-quizen.»; in progress «Fortsett Replay, Uke 9.»; AI-held Seats named «KI-spiller» from first view; finished shows done plus next step available. A closed Replay says it was closed at the Testfrist and is not finished.
- Replay uses the Epic 6 play screen unchanged (Kjedebånd, Graf over tabell, Ordrefelt, Setestatus), with no Pausevalg or «Sett inn KI-spiller»; AI rows show «venter» while waiting. Only the Replay adds the dashed `projection` line ending in a hollow dot at nå + 4, with the sentence «Den stiplede linja viser netto lager om fire uker: 3 + 24 − 4 × 8 = −5 hvis ordre inn holder seg. Ikke garantert.» The line is `aria-hidden`, distinguished by dash and marker, not colour alone; the sentence is the text alternative; it wraps without overlap at 320 px.
- Only a Retailer sees customer demand, one Week at a time. Replay-gjennomgang states: pending «Gjennomgangen lages.» in a Skjelett of the chart; ready; code fallback with the same chart and numbers. Rating scale is a labelled radio group, keyboard operable, options at least 44 px, end points labelled in words. Finish screen has one primary button and uses the shared number formatter.
