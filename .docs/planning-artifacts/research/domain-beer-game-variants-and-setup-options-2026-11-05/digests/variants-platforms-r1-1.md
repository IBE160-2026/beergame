## Leads worth chasing

- Forio "The Beer Game Online: Facilitator Guide (PDF)" (behind login, under "Teaching Materials"); is the authoritative list of MIT/Forio settings and whether game length is hidden. Support: support@forio.com.
- Sterman (1989) Management Science 35(3):321-339 and Business Dynamics (2000) chapter on the Beer Game; needed to settle the order-delay 1 vs 2 weeks question and the factory production delay (3 or 4). The Forrester 1961 Industrial Dynamics model equations too.
- Croson & Donohue (2006) and Narayanan & Moritz (2015) experiments, cited by arXiv 2202.12786, for rule parameter sets used in research variants.
- Lee, Padmanabhan, Whang (1997) "The Bullwhip Effect in Supply Chains" for the information-sharing and stochastic-demand lineage (not retrieved).
- Zensimu changelogs (feedback.zensimu.com): "lead time variability" (2024-02-10) and schedule events (2024-03-11); and Zensimu "best beer game simulations comparison guide" (competitor view of other platforms).
- github.com/vrjparikh/Game (IIM-A multi-team web Beer Game), wirelessr/beer_game repo README, and transentis Business Prototyping Toolkit source for config options.
- Darden "The Beer Game (SIMULATION)" (store.darden.virginia.edu) and Octigo sims.octigo.pl "logistics game", seen in search results, not read.
- Wikipedia's references: transentis "Understanding the Beer Game" (2015) and Supply Chain Academy.
