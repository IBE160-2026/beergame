# Voice-of-customer digest, round 1, agent e (2026-10-26)

Scope: customer voice on beer game tools and the classic manual game. 11 pages retrieved and read; 2 more (Harvard HBP, Wisconsin poster) used only via search-result snippets (fetch returned 403 / unreadable PDF), flagged as such. Reddit is blocked for both search and fetch in this environment, so no Reddit evidence was gathered. Almost all material is educator-authored or vendor-authored. No genuine student voice and no app-store or G2 reviews were retrieved.

## Findings

### Complaints about the classic / manual game and browser tools
- Educator says students "concentrate hard, but not always on the right things", mechanics (moving tokens, checking arithmetic) buried the learning; game is "slow to run and proves surprisingly fragile"; "small errors in tracking orders or inventory can distort results" | https://timeshighereducation.com/campus/less-beer-more-couscous-gamifying-supply-chain-education | Times Higher Education Campus (Jawad Abrache, prof. of operations research, Al Akhawayn Univ., Morocco) | 2026-06-07 | accessed 2026-10-26 | high (primary, n=1 educator) | sentiment
- Fixed demand pattern lets students anticipate outcomes after a round or two (so replay value of the standard script is low) | same THE URL | THE Campus | 2026-06-07 | accessed 2026-10-26 | high (n=1) | need
- Debrief difficulty: students understood the outcome by the debrief but "few could clearly explain how their decisions had led them there" | same THE URL | THE Campus | 2026-06-07 | accessed 2026-10-26 | high (n=1) | sentiment
- What the author built instead: a "Couscous Game", browser-based, automates orders/inventories/costs/delays, varies demand (mild and abrupt), phased sessions (mechanics, team competition, system-wide analysis, coordination); a capstone student later built a multiplayer version; open source and online. Localised reference point (couscous) makes it less abstract. Article does not name any commercial tool the author tried and gives no class sizes | same THE URL | THE Campus | 2026-06-07 | accessed 2026-10-26 | high | feature
- Large classes: running the manual game for ~400 undergrads has "extensive administrative challenges"; authors say computer versions are easier to administer but "inhibit the optimal learning opportunities" of the manual game (abstract only; absentees not discussed) | https://ucalgary.scholaris.ca/handle/1880/51105?mode=full | Decision Sciences Institute annual meeting, Snider/Balakrishnan/da Silveira (Univ. of Calgary repository record) | 2010 | accessed 2026-10-26 | med (abstract only; STALE) | sentiment
- Attendance: before going online, up to 25% of students had conflicts (other courses, illness, commute, sports) and missed the face-to-face game; face-to-face depended on a 400-seat room; fix was an online version using Responsive's simulation, piloted Fall 2018 (survey results not on the page) | https://ucalgary.scholaris.ca/handle/1880/110564?show=full | Univ. of Calgary repository, poster by Maria Stoletova (Conf. on Postsecondary Learning and Teaching) | 2019-04-30 | accessed 2026-10-26 | high for the 25% figure as reported by the author (n=1 course; STALE) | need
- Manual-vs-online learning quality: a Univ. of Wisconsin-Superior study reportedly found the game-board version "more valuable" for understanding bullwhip than the online version, though it needs more time and orchestration (snippet only, PDF unreadable) | https://www.wisconsin.edu/opid/download/wtfs_reports/wtfs_16-17/Cao-poster-WTFS-2016-17.pdf | Univ. of Wisconsin system teaching-fellows poster (Cao) | 2016-17 | accessed 2026-10-26 | low (snippet only; STALE) | sentiment

### Not enough players / no-shows / dropouts
- Harvard Business Publishing guidance for the Root Beer Game in a virtual classroom: every user assigned to a chain must actively participate or the chain cannot proceed; advises inactivating no-show students so they are not assigned a role, and randomly assigning students who are online. Workaround is manual instructor effort | https://help.hbsp.harvard.edu/hc/en-us/articles/6157823105555-Using-the-Supply-Chain-Management-Simulation-Root-Beer-Game-in-a-Virtual-Classroom | Harvard Business Publishing help centre | undated | accessed 2026-10-26 (via search snippet; page returned 403) | med (vendor guidance, indirectly evidences the problem) | need
- Zensimu documents its workarounds: the Computer fills any unfilled role; auto-expanding teams; timer lets the Computer act for a player who does not submit; in person two people can share a role (max 2 recommended); non-multiples of 4 are handled by Computer players. This confirms the problem is common enough that the vendor built features and a guide section around it | https://zensimu.com/resources/large-sessions-guide/ | Zensimu (vendor) | undated | accessed 2026-10-26 | med (vendor, no frequency data) | feature
- Zensimu case-study page: if a student leaves or disconnects the computer takes over the role; players assigned randomly or from an Excel sheet; a game can be launched before the final player count is known | https://zensimu.com/resources/the-beergame-app-and-universities/ | Zensimu (vendor) | undated | accessed 2026-10-26 | med (marketing register) | feature
- Quality of the fallback: one reviewer on GetApp/Software Advice reportedly noted that when there were not enough players "the computer did not play very well" (seen only in a search summary; both review pages returned 403 so I could not read the review or its date) | https://www.getapp.de/software/2061710/zensimu | GetApp (Gartner Digital Markets) | unknown | accessed 2026-10-26 | low (second-hand summary, n=1) | sentiment
- Fathomd (2021) added an asynchronous Beer Distribution Game: groups of up to 4 play at a time of their choosing, as homework or instead of in-class play, for "better scheduling flexibility" | https://fathomd.com/latest-news/2021/9/8/announcing-new-fathomd-games | Fathomd (vendor) | 2021-09-08 | accessed 2026-10-26 | med as a signal that scheduling is a pain point; no demand data (STALE) | feature

## Evidence counts (honest)
- Independent educator voice retrieved: 1 detailed (THE 2026), 2 academic abstracts (Calgary 2010, 2019, stale), 1 snippet (Wisconsin 2016-17). Student voice: 0. Reddit voice: 0 (domain blocked). HN: 1 Show HN with no outside feedback plus a handful of general-discussion comments.
