## Cross-cutting observations
- None of the three shows LLM-driven players or AI-generated debrief in anything I retrieved. "AI players"/"ghost"/"server plays position" are best read as scripted fallback bots; none documents the policy.
- Solo mode exists only at transentis (documented, free). Demand variability is undocumented at all three. Debrief is strongest at transentis (dashboard, exports), instructor/class tooling strongest at eBeer (teams, trial/course accounts, manual sales process), weakest at MA-system (consulting-led).
- Business models differ: transentis free lead-gen for consulting; MA-system consulting service with game as demo vehicle; eBeer paid per-student ($10-20), manual sales.
