## Leads worth chasing
- En Dash multiplayer AI: the server (WebSocket endpoint) was not inspected; hands-on play of a mixed human/AI session would reveal whether server-side bots use the same heuristic or an LLM. Also play the solo route and step through `/games/:id/replay` to verify replay controls.
- En Dash bots are naive (order = incoming + 0.5 x gap, +/-15% noise); a rival with smarter/varied AI personas (e.g. over-reactive, anchoring, base-stock) and a post-game comparison "you vs each bot style" would differentiate.
- En Dash has no random demand, no stated LMS, no terms/privacy pages, and is a consulting lead magnet; the "Request a demo" funnel for hosted setups suggests possible paid workshops.
- Skill Dynamics: submit the lead form (needs business email; outward-facing action, not done) to reach the actual game URL and inspect bots.
- Couscous Game: search Springer / Al Akhawayn pages / ResearchGate for the paper and repo; contact author (reported colleague interest means possible forks).
- Eureka: try the demo or the `/beergame/` overview page to check for bot fill or solo mode.
