## Synthesis
- No product found that ships LLM-driven AI players for empty beer-game roles with a solo-replay experience. Closest: an abandoned HF Space (human Distributor + GPT-4o-mini bots) and all-LLM research demos with no human seat.
- Rule-based/RL empty-role bots exist in transentis (free) and Forio ($168/person) but their behavior/difficulty is undocumented.
- LLM agents in the beer game often show severe bullwhip (GenAI Beer Game, Happily), while a 2026 paper says best configs beat humans by up to 80%; i.e. LLM-as-opponent quality is volatile and needs guardrails.
