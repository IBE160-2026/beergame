# Red-team R3-N: adversarial check of the "no LLM-opponent / no LLM-debrief beer game" claim

Claim under test: "As of October 2026 no beer game product or open-source project lets a human learner play solo or replay against LLM/generative-AI-driven opponents filling the other roles, and no beer-game product offers an LLM-generated debrief or AI coach."
Weaker related claim: "Several products already fill empty roles with non-LLM computer players."
Accessed all sources 2026-10-26. About 14 distinct sources and about 30 tool calls.

Verdict on the weaker claim: it holds. At least four fetched sources (endash, Skill Dynamics, transentis, Zensimu, plus eBeer's server-played positions) fill empty roles with a computer player. None states LLM use. Two of the seven named in the brief (Forio, MA-system) could not be confirmed from the sources I fetched. For endash, Skill Dynamics and transentis the mechanism ("AI") is undisclosed, so "non-LLM" is an inference for them. It is firmly stated only for Zensimu (a formula), eBeer (a server position) and the transentis notebook (RL, not tied to the web game).

## C. Verdict

The conclusion as worded fails on its strongest clause and survives in a narrower form. The Hugging Face Space Lilli98/Beergamesimulation is a counterexample: an open-source Streamlit app where a human plays the Distributor against Retailer, Wholesaler and Factory agents driven by GPT-4o-mini, with an LLM-generated order suggestion for the human. It was created in September 2025 and updated in November 2025. It is a research-experiment interface (participant IDs, logs pushed to a HF dataset) and was in `BUILD_ERROR` when queried, so it is not a maintained learner product. The main commercial and educational candidates do not show LLM opponents. En Dash (Aug 2026) and Skill Dynamics offer a solo seat with "AI" roles but never say what the AI is, and the Harvard/MIT/Georgia Tech GenAI Beer Game (Aug 2025) is LLM-only with no human seat. I found no beer-game product with an LLM-generated debrief or AI coach. En Dash's "insight" is hard-coded text and its debrief is a data readout. The only coach-like LLM feature is the experiment's in-game suggestion. Residual risks: En Dash's and Skill Dynamics' server-side AI is opaque and could use an LLM; GPT Store, Gem and Claude-artifact libraries are poorly indexed by web search, so I cannot rule out vibe-coded versions there; and I did not run Norwegian, Swedish, Danish or French queries, or App Store and Google Play searches. A defensible restatement: "As of 2026-10-26 no maintained, learner-facing beer game product is documented as using LLM-driven opponents with a human seat or an LLM-generated debrief. Only research prototypes (an HF Streamlit experiment with GPT-4o-mini opponents and an LLM suggestion) and LLM-only simulations exist."
