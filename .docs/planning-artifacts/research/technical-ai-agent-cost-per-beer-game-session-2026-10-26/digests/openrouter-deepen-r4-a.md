# openrouter-deepen-r4-a (technical, Deepen: OpenRouter as AI provider)

Accessed: 2026-10-26 for all rows. Done by the lead directly (not a subagent), 14 WebFetch + 1 WebSearch calls on openrouter.ai official docs/pages. Caveat: WebFetch returns a small-model summary of each page, so quotes are "as relayed". No page except the privacy policy shows a date.
Question owned: does OpenRouter change the cost, reproducibility, reliability or privacy conclusions of the technical report?

## Findings (claim | URL | publisher | pub_date | confidence | class)

1. Billing: "Inference is billed at each provider's list price on every plan." Platform fee applies only when buying credits: Standard 5.5%, Business 8%, Enterprise by contract, Free none. BYOK: Standard/Business include $25,000 of list-price inference per month with no OpenRouter fee; above that 5% of list price. Errored attempts "not charged for model tokens" under fallback routing. Taxes exclusive. | https://openrouter.ai/pricing | OpenRouter | undated | medium | pricing
2. Free tier: 25+ free models, capped at 20 requests/minute and 50/day; daily cap 1,000 after buying at least $10 credits. Free: 5 workspaces. | https://openrouter.ai/pricing ; https://openrouter.ai/docs/api-reference/limits | OpenRouter | undated | medium | pricing / doc-guarantee
3. Plans: Business adds EU/US in-region routing; Enterprise adds SSO/SAML, contractual SLAs, custom BYOK limits; Standard has "passthrough provider rate limits". | pricing page | OpenRouter | undated | medium | pricing
4. Haiku 5.5 model page: EU in-region prices $0.11/M input, $0.55/M output via Google Vertex and Amazon Bedrock; EU routing requires Business or Enterprise via eu.openrouter.ai. Fetch of the page did not show non-EU prices, cache prices, context length, temperature/seed support, uptime stats. A search summary of OpenRouter pages (model page, :batch page, compare pages) states standard $0.10 in / $0.50 out, cache read $0.01, write $0.125 (5 min) / $0.20 (1 h), batch variant $0.05/$0.25, 1M context (search summary only; matches Anthropic list price). Same search shows the alias page "Claude Haiku Latest" still at $1/$5 (Haiku 4.5), i.e. aliases can lag. | https://openrouter.ai/anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5 ; https://openrouter.ai/anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5:batch ; https://openrouter.ai/~anthropic/claude-haiku-latest/api | OpenRouter | undated | medium (EU prices), low-medium (others, search summary) | pricing

Parameters: temperature 0.0-2.0, top_p, top_k, seed, max_tokens, response_format, structured_outputs, tools, reasoning, reasoning_effort, verbosity etc. Seed: "sample deterministically" when specified; "Determinism is not guaranteed for some models." Temperature: "At 0, the model always gives the same response for a given input." No per-model support table; read the model's provider section. | https://openrouter.ai/docs/api-reference/parameters | OpenRouter | undated | medium | docs-guarantee
6. Unsupported parameters are ignored, not rejected: "then the parameter is ignored"; others forwarded to the underlying API. | https://openrouter.ai/docs/api-reference/overview | OpenRouter | undated | medium | docs-guarantee
7. Provider routing: default load balancing across providers favouring lower price (inverse square of price) after deprioritising recent outages; `sort` price/throughput/latency; `allow_fallbacks` default true; `order`, `only`, `ignore`; `quantizations`; `require_parameters`: "Only use providers that support all parameters in your request"; `data_collection` default "allow" (providers may store non-transiently and train), "deny" restricts; `zdr: true` restricts to Zero Data Retention endpoints. No discussion of nondeterminism or reproducibility across providers; quantised models "may exhibit degraded performance"; targeting a specific endpoint helps to "consistently use a particular variant". | https://openrouter.ai/docs/features/provider-routing | OpenRouter | undated | medium | docs-guarantee
8. Model fallbacks (`models` array): "any error can trigger the use of a fallback model" (context length, moderation, rate limit, downtime); billed at the model ultimately used; the answering model is in the response `model` field. | https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/model-fallbacks | OpenRouter | undated | medium | docs-guarantee
9. Prompt caching: Anthropic needs `cache_control` (top-level automatic or per block), write 1.25x (5 min) / 2x (1 h), read 0.1x, minimum "1,024 to 4,096 tokens, depending on model" (conflicts with Anthropic's own page: 512 tokens for Haiku 5.5, see main report [2]); OpenAI/DeepSeek/Gemini implicit automatic; provider stickiness after a cached request, session expires after 10 minutes of inactivity, skipped with manual `provider.order`; `session_id` sets the routing key. | https://openrouter.ai/docs/features/prompt-caching | OpenRouter | undated | medium | docs-guarantee
10. Structured outputs: `response_format` type json_schema, `strict: true`; enforcement provider-dependent ("exact compliance isn't guaranteed everywhere"); support is per endpoint and can change; to guarantee, use `require_parameters: true`; Response Healing plugin for non-streaming; unsupported model fails with an error. | https://openrouter.ai/docs/features/structured-outputs | OpenRouter | undated | medium | docs-guarantee
11. Reasoning: `reasoning.effort` max..none ("none" disables), `reasoning.max_tokens` (Anthropic min 1024), `exclude`; reasoning tokens billed as output even when excluded; counts against `max_tokens` (can end with finish_reason "length" and empty content); some models mandatory reasoning reject "none"; Anthropic `thinking: {type: "disabled"}` turns reasoning off. | https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/best-practices/reasoning-tokens | OpenRouter | undated | medium | docs-guarantee
12. Rate limits: paid models have "no platform-level request cap"; provider-side limits still apply; negative balance causes errors; prepaid accounts below an unspecified balance threshold get an in-flight spending budget (unspecified); 429 may come from OpenRouter or the provider; retry with backoff; `GET /api/v1/key` shows limits and usage. | https://openrouter.ai/docs/api-reference/limits | OpenRouter | undated | medium | doc-guarantee
13. Usage accounting: usage always returned in non-streaming responses (final chunk when streaming), native tokenizer counts, `cost`, `cost_details`, `is_byok`; stats retrievable via `/api/v1/generation`. Failover to other providers on 5xx. No latency overhead, uptime percentages or SLA stated in the page read. | https://openrouter.ai/docs/api-reference/overview | OpenRouter | undated | medium | doc-guarantee
14. Privacy (guide): "OpenRouter does not store your prompts or responses, unless you opt in"; never shared/sold/licensed prompt data; opt-in to let OpenRouter use inputs/outputs gives a 1% discount; request metadata (token counts, latency) is stored; ZDR/EU residency not covered on this page. | https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/privacy/data-collection | OpenRouter | undated | medium | doc-guarantee
15. Privacy policy (Last Updated August 31, 2026): never uses "controller"/"processor"; "We do not control, and are not responsible for, LLMs' handling of your Inputs or Outputs"; Model Providers may retain and use Inputs for their own purposes; data may be transferred outside EEA/UK under adequacy decisions / SCCs (Art. 45/46); no specific retention period for text prompts; subprocessors named: Google Analytics, PostHog; "If you have a Data Processing Agreement with us" its terms govern provider handling (DPA exists for some users). | https://openrouter.ai/privacy | OpenRouter, Inc. | 2026-08-31 | medium-high | doc-guarantee
16. ZDR: enabled account-wide (privacy settings, per model group), per guardrail, or per request (`zdr: true`, which can add but not remove enforcement); endpoint list at /api/v1/endpoints/zdr; unclear policy => assumed to retain and train; ZDR removes first-party Anthropic endpoints (Bedrock and Vertex remain), first-party OpenAI (Azure remains), AI Studio (Vertex remains); price effect not stated; availability may shrink; implicit in-memory caching not treated as retention. | https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/features/zdr | OpenRouter | undated | medium | doc-guarantee
