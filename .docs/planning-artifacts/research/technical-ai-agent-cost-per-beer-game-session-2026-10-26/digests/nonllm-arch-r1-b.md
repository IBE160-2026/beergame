### RL (Dimension 2, Q1)
1. The DQN paper ("SRDQN") is by Oroojlooyjadid, Nazari, Snyder, Takac (Lehigh); it exists only as arXiv preprint in the pages I read: v1 20 Aug 2017, v2 8 Mar 2018, v3 7 Feb 2019, v4 14 Oct 2020; arXiv abs page lists no journal reference. 2. Training is slow: paper says "the training of the SRDQN algorithm can be slow"; up to 60,000 episodes per agent, replay memory of the one million most recent experiences; network fully connected [5m,180,130,61,au-al+1], m=5 or 10 in basic setup. | https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1708.05924 | arXiv/ar5iv mirror of preprint | v4 2020-10 | 2026-10-26 | medium (fetched via small-model summarizer, not verbatim table) | performance-number
3. Hyperparameter tuning for the base agent took 28,390,987 seconds in total across 140 instances (~329 CPU/GPU-days summed over instances; hardware not stated in the text I saw). Transfer learning needed 12 instances averaging 1,613,711 seconds, "about 17.6 times faster". My arithmetic: 28,390,987 s / 86,400 = ~328.6 days total over 140 instances (~2.3 days per instance). | same ar5iv URL | arXiv preprint | 2020-10 | 2026-10-26 | medium | performance-number
4. Inference cost: "once trained, our algorithm executes nearly instantaneously"; no measured latency given. Network is small (MLP, a few hundred units per layer), so browser/tiny-server inference is plausible BUT this is my inference from architecture, not a claim in the paper. | same | arXiv preprint | 2020-10 | 2026-10-26 | medium (quote) / low (browser feasibility) | other
5. Behavioural realism: the DQN agent is trained to MINIMISE cost, not imitate humans. With base-stock teammates it is near-optimal (average gap 2.31% vs optimal base-stock in the basic case; 8.15% uniform / 5.80% normal demand in literature cases). With Sterman-formula teammates it is ~23% better than the optimised base-stock on average (literature cases). Paper does not claim human-like play; the human-like element is the teammates (Sterman formula agents). So an RL agent as a stand-in "supplier/retailer" would be a cost-minimiser, not a myopic human. | https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1708.05924 | arXiv preprint | 2020-10 | 2026-10-26 | medium | performance-number
6. Only ONE SRDQN agent is tested at a time, playing with non-RL teammates (base-stock, Sterman, or random "d+x"); multi-RL-agent training left to future work. Limitations stated: no theoretical optimality guarantee; needs training data or demand distribution; reward-shaping coefficient beta_i must be cross-validated; larger unscaled demands: costs within 10% avg, always within 20% of optimised base-stock; performance degrades most when stockout cost falls while holding cost rises. | same | arXiv preprint | 2020-10 | 2026-10-26 | medium | other
7. Abstract also says authors propose transfer learning to adapt trained agents to other agents/cost settings, relevant to per-session adaptation cost. | https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.05924v4 | arXiv | 2020-10-14 | 2026-10-26 | high | other

### Determinism / architecture (Dimension 3, Q3)
12. OpenAI: seed gives "best effort to sample deterministically"; "Determinism is not guaranteed"; system_fingerprint identifies backend config and changes with request params or infrastructure updates; "small chance that responses differ even when request parameters and system_fingerprint match"; feature "in beta" and limited to specific models. Page undated, marked archived, "may reference outdated models or APIs" so current API-reference behaviour not verified. | https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/reproducible_outputs_with_the_seed_parameter | OpenAI developers cookbook | undated (archived) | 2026-10-26 | high (that guarantee is explicitly absent) / low (currency) | docs-guarantee
13. Anthropic glossary: "Even with temperature set to 0, the results will not be fully deterministic, and identical inputs might produce different outputs across API calls. This applies both to Anthropic's first-party inference service and to inference through third-party cloud providers." Glossary also says the Claude API "does not currently offer fine-tuning". Glossary mentions Claude 4.7+ tokenizer ~30% more tokens than earlier models (relevant to cost estimates). Page date not shown. Seed parameter: none seen on the glossary or the visible part of the Messages API param list (max_tokens, messages, model, cache_control, container, diagnostics, inference_geo, metadata, output_config, service_tier, speed, stop_sequences, stream, system, thinking, tool_choice, tools), but only first ~100k of 1.29M chars read, so "no seed param" is NOT confirmed. | https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/glossary | Anthropic | undated (fetched live 2026-10-26) | 2026-10-26 | high (quote) | docs-guarantee
14. Failure report: Atil et al. test five LLMs on eight tasks, 10 runs, "deterministic" settings: accuracy varies by up to 15% across runs, best-worst gap up to 70%; no LLM consistently repeatable across all tasks; identical output strings rarer than identical parsed answers. arXiv preprint (cs.CL), v1 6 Aug 2024, v5 2 Apr 2025, no venue listed. Abstract read only. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04667 | arXiv | 2025-04-02 (v5) | 2026-10-26 | high (abstract numbers) | failure-report
15. Search-snippet only (not fetched, unverified): arXiv 2407.10457 ("Evaluation of LLMs Should Not Ignore Non-Determinism") and 2308.02828 (ChatGPT code-generation non-determinism, temperature 0 improves but does not eliminate variation); a Thinking Machines Lab report cited as 80 unique completions in 1000 temp-0 runs, fixed by batch-invariant kernels (via a secondary paper's description). | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10457 ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.02828 | arXiv | 2024 / 2023 | 2026-10-26 | low | failure-report
16. Implication for architecture (my inference, not sourced): since neither vendor guarantees determinism at temperature 0/seed, reproducible replays require storing the realised agent decisions (cache/record-replay) or using non-LLM agents for turn play; sampling a local deterministic policy (rule/RL with explicit RNG seed) is reproducible by construction. Mark as design reasoning, medium.
