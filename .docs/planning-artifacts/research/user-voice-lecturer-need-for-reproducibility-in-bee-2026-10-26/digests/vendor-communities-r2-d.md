## Language observed (educator/vendor terms)
- "debrief"/"debriefing" dominant (Zensimu, THE, ASCM Richmond event page in results). Confirms round 1.
- "demand pattern" (Forio/Transentis settings, Zensimu comparison); "consumer demand"/"final consumer demand" (Zensimu, beergame.org); "demand series" (beergame.org); "predefined change/jump" (beergame.org).
- "consistency across your organization" (Zensimu, about templates); "saved settings"/"saved settings preset" (beergame.org / Transentis); "shared templates", "saved sessions" (Zensimu).
- "anticipate outcomes" (THE) = the educator-side phrasing of the memorisation/predictability worry.
- NOT found in any source: "seed", "same demand for all teams", "reproducible", "replay", "fair comparison across sections", "cheating", "memorize".

## Market signal (question 4)
No product read advertises identical demand across teams, seeded demand, or scenario reproducibility. Closest: Zensimu "consistency across your organization" via shared templates (item 4) and implicit shared session settings (item 2); beergame.org's fixed typed-in demand series + saved settings (item 8). Absence is the finding, but only 4-5 vendor pages read; not exhaustive. Competitive angle: vendors differentiate on customisation/monitoring/debrief, not on reproducibility.
