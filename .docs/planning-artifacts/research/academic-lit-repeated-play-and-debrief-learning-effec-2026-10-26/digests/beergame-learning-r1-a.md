## Bottom line for the decision (honest read)

- Verified this run: (a) people show persistent misunderstanding of delays and stocks/flows even in simple beer game conditions (items 1, 2), and (b) one peer-reviewed abstract (Hong & Jeong 2011) says repeated play of an online BDSG improved results, with no reported n or effect size and no conceptual-understanding measure (item 3).
- NOT verified: that repeated play improves understanding (as opposed to performance on a similar task), that it is not memorisation of a fixed demand pattern, or that it transfers. I found no paper this run that tests transfer or measures students' ability to explain causes before/after (Q3). So the product's core premise is, on my evidence, an untested hypothesis requiring their own measurement.
- No evidence found of AI debrief or AI tutor effects on beer game learning (Q4). LLM agents as players exist only as 2026 preprints about agent performance, not learning.
