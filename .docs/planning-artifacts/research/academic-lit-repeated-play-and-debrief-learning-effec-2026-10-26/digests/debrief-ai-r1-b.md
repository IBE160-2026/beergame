Run date / access date for all items: 2026-10-26. Searcher: research assistant r1-b.

Questions owned: (1) meta-analyses/surveys on simulation-game effectiveness and debriefing; (2) controlled studies isolating debrief vs no debrief and repeated vs single play; (3) transfer to dynamic-decision / systems-thinking tasks; (4) AI/LLM feedback, automated debriefs, AI tutors in simulations.

## Headline for the decision
- No source found this run isolates debriefing from repetition in the same design, and none tests AI-generated debriefs against human debriefs or no debrief with learning outcomes. The two effects are studied in separate literatures.
- Moderator evidence from meta-analyses favours repetition/replay and supplementation with other instruction, but both meta-analyses abstracts say nothing about debriefing as a moderator.
- The Sterman/Paich line (dynamic-decision tasks closest to the Beer Game) finds repetition alone improved average performance without insight; this is the strongest direct counterweight to "repeat play = understanding".

### C. Repetition / dynamic-decision transfer (closest to Beer Game)

9.
