## Net assessment for the decision
- Nothing opened in this round shows (a) n, (b) whether demand repeated identically across trials, or (c) any understanding or transfer measure, for beer-game repeated play. The fixed-demand confound remains unexamined by any source I could read.
- The literature as characterised by secondary sources is split: Sterman 1989 and Diehl & Sterman 1995 (as cited by others) say learning is poor or plateaus; CMU DDM Lab (Martin 2004, Fu & Gonzalez 2006, as summarised by a search engine) say practice improves performance. Both are cost/performance outcomes; none confirmed as conceptual understanding.
- Do not cite Martin 2004 or Fu & Gonzalez 2006 for any number or design detail from this round.
