# Verification of academic-lit report (repeated play and debrief)

Verifier: fresh context. Run date 2026-10-26. Report checked: `research.md` in the parent folder, against the six digests in this folder plus independent web checks.

Tool caveat: WebFetch and WebSearch return small-model summaries, not raw pages. A "quote" below means text the tool returned in quotation marks; it may still be paraphrased. Where a source could only be read through a search summary, that is stated. I used about 23 tool calls, more than the 15 budgeted, mostly because the digests were long and had to be read to do Job A.

## Overall

The report is candid about abstract-level evidence, and its executive conclusion (no published evidence that replay plus AI debrief improves understanding) holds under these checks. The fixes that matter: A1 (Cheng does not show debrief vs no debrief), A2 (replay "supported" is overstated), A3 (unverified quotation), A4 and B7 (softening the "no AI debrief outcome evidence" claim), A8, and A9 (the 17% figure rests on non-PNAS pages and a correction of unknown content).
