## Independent review in the founder's place

Independent review agent (autonomous build of Story 15.13), 2026-11-22, with Playwright CLI 0.1.24 (real Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space and Escape presses) and axe-core 4.14.0. The build ran with no human in the loop, so an independent review agent did the keyboard, focus and axe checks the founder would have made. It does not close any check above: an agent cannot use a screen reader.

24 screens of every Role (public pages, Student Hjem, play screen, Regler, Debrief, Replay, Lecturer Lærerhjem, Klasse, Klasseøkt, Oversikt, Spillinnsikt, and the seven Admin pages) were walked with the keyboard at 1440 and 320 px, and checked with axe at both widths and for sideways scroll at 320 px. Every control was reachable in visual order, the focus ring (2 px solid navy, 2 px offset) was visible on each, no keyboard trap was found, toggles and disclosures worked with Enter and Space, and the footer link was the last tab stop on every screen. Every screen has lang="nb", one h1, one main landmark, a banner and a footer. axe found no violation; SVG chart text that axe could not decide was measured by hand at 4.51:1 or more. No defect was found.

Observations (none is a WCAG 2.1 A/AA failure):

- The inline panels on Klasse, Klasseøkt and the Student's Hjem («Legg til adresse», «Registrer manuelt», «Ny Labtime», «Foreslå tidspunkt») do not close on Escape; «Avbryt» closes them and returns focus to the opener. They are inline content, not dialogs.
- There is no skip link. Bypass blocks (2.4.1) is met through the banner and main landmarks; the Lecturer screens have a two- to four-link breadcrumb before the content.
- The Matrisevegg's cells are 32 × 18 px on the Landing and 36 × 18 px on the Oversikt. WCAG 2.1 AA sets no target size (2.5.8 is WCAG 2.2).
- The order field's − and + buttons are named «Én færre» and «Én flere»; symbols are not text labels for 2.5.3, so this passes, but a person may want to confirm it.
- Spillinnsikt: axe left th-has-data-cells undecided for one table; no table with header cells only was found.
- The Landing's lowest SVG text contrast is 4.51:1, just above the 4.5:1 limit.

Not checked by the review:

- Screen reader output (no screen reader available to the agent).
- Buttons that change state (Bekreft ordre, Pause spillet, Sett inn KI-spiller, delete, deactivate, archive, send) were not pressed, so any confirmation step they open was not walked.
- Error states after a failed submit, empty and very long lists, forced colours, dark mode and reduced motion.
