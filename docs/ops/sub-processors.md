# Sub-processor register

Started by Story 15.11 for the DPIA pack (PRD §6.1, spine Deferred «Sub-processor register and data processing agreement gate»). It lists every outside service that receives data from ØL-spillet in production: what it receives, where, and on what basis a transfer outside the EU/EEA rests. Revise it in place when a service, a region or a basis changes; the readiness record (`docs/ops/readiness.md`) has the item that confirms it before the pilot.

**Status: started, not confirmed.** The regions are the ones the setup guide asks for. Every transfer basis below is the expected one and must be confirmed against the provider's current data processing agreement (DPA) by the founder, who records the date in the readiness record. Rows marked *open* wait for a founder decision (issue #28).
